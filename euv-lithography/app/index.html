ACCURACY BOUNDARY

A published reference. An illustrated process.

The scanner cycle is calibrated to the published 220 wafers/hour configuration at 30 mJ/cm². Scan time is 117 ms per field. Field-step time and the 1.2 s stage exchange are estimates. The cycle is not an independent prediction of throughput.

The optics close view uses a ray overlay to show the path through the cutaway. The light bundle follows the mirror centres. It does not solve imaging, diffraction, dose, or collisions. Ray colour is illustrative. The complete wafer route does not show a validated production recipe.
